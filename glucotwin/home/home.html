The platform

One twin, three layers of data.

A patient's glucose depends on more than the last reading. The twin weighs recent readings, daily activity and long-term health together.

01.

Glucose (CGM)

Recent levels, trends, variability and time of day, from a reading every five minutes.

02.

Wearables

Heart rate, steps and sleep from a watch, plus logged meals and insulin.

03.

Health record

Age, diabetes type and duration, HbA1c, kidney function, medications and past lows.

About the prototype

A research prototype for two-hour glucose risk.

GlucoTwin forecasts glucose two hours ahead, estimates the chance of a low or a high, and lists the inputs behind every alert. It runs on a synthetic cohort by default and can replay public CGM data such as GlucoFM-Bench. It is an educational prototype, not a medical device, and has not been clinically validated.
